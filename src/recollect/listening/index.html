About this comparison

A/B order and the shorter comparisons are shuffled for each session. The long listen comes last; take a break whenever you need one. Repeat the suite another day using “New session.” These are the same recordings, presented in a new order.

Recordings preserve synthesis chunk boundaries, with no live network or playback stalls. They do not measure live chat responsiveness. Audio has not been loudness-normalized. Listen to both clips before saving; replay freely. The short reply also checks whether a small or absent difference is perceived.

Identities stay hidden unless you reveal them. A reveal is recorded in your export, so those ratings can be distinguished from blind ratings.
